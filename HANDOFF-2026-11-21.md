## PROVEN facts (do not re-test)

1. dArkOS's initramfs **hardcodes `/dev/mmcblk1p4`** as root → any rootfs there boots with their kernel.
2. The loader **reads `extlinux.conf`** but overrides `root=` (to `PARTUUID=614e0000-0000`) and `console=` (to `console=console=ttyUSB0,115200`); everything else in `APPEND` survives.
3. u-boot env (from dArkOS `uboot.img`): `kernel_addr_r=0x00a80000`, `ramdisk_addr_r=0x0a200000`,
   `fdt_addr_r=0x0a100000`, `bootcmd=boot_fit; boot_android; …distro_bootcmd`, `boot_targets=mmc1 mmc0 usb0 pxe dhcp`.
4. **ROCKNIX 7.2.11 `Image` does not reach `init`** on a *clean* card: no tracer, no dmesg, no userspace.
   Most likely ATF/PSCI mismatch (dArkOS `bl31` vs ROCKNIX's). Unconfirmed — needs UART.
5. Kernel `CONFIG_KEXEC` is **not set** → no eMMC chain-load to mainline.
6. `ext4` is built into the ROCKNIX kernel; Arch-R userland targets Linux **6.10+** (busybox `chmod` → `ENOSYS` on 5.10).
7. ROCKNIX will not boot while Android is on the eMMC — official wiki: wipe the Android partition
   (power+vol-down → recovery → `adb root` → `dd if=/dev/zero of=/dev/block/mmcblk0 bs=4M`).
   **User decision: keep Android (needed for the andr36oid port) → mainline/panfrost is out of scope.**
8. Panel: DSI binds and the framebuffer works; the "white screen" was a stale scanout page + low backlight
   (`echo 120 > /sys/class/backlight/backlight/brightness`).

## Hard-won operational rules

- **Never power off by holding the button.** Hard cuts corrupted the ext4 rootfs (bitmap checksums) and the
  FAT (resurrected 8.3 entries, truncated kernel copies). Use `poweroff`/`reboot` over SSH.
- Keep p3 half-free; never leave duplicate kernels/initrds there.
- After a long write, do **not** build verification loops: `fsck` + device boot is the test.
- If `chmod` fails in the Arch rootfs, use `python3 -c "import os; os.chmod(p,0o755)"` (busybox/glibc ABI gap).
- `systemctl reboot --no-block` over SSH; commands in a background subshell die with the session.
